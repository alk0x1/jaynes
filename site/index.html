How it works

Four layers between your agents and the world.

The method makes skipping impossible. Checks catch what a machine can see. Your answers decide where your attention goes. A gate underneath keeps every action in bounds.

1 · Method

The agent cannot skip a step.

A role is a sequence of steps, each with a required output and a check. Jaynes runs Claude Code or Codex for one step at a time, with write access to that step's output and nothing else. Evidence the agent could invent, like a walkthrough of a screen, is produced by Jaynes.

Press Run the method to send one task through the screen-design role.
2 · Checks

Checks first, then your call.

A delivery reaches you only after passing what a machine can verify: tests written before the code, contrast, design tokens, and a comparison with the last version you approved. You decide the rest, and your note goes back to the agent.

3 · Attention

Your attention moves to where it is still needed.

Every answer at a stop is a label. Keep accepting a stop without changes and it becomes sampled: it asks one time in ten. That takes 20 accepts in a row at a 20% budget, 43 at 10%, 88 at 5%. Change a sampled answer and it asks every time again.

4 · Safety · works today

Every action goes through a gate.

Each tool call is classified before it runs. Reading runs. Pushing to a remote, deleting history or sending a message never runs without you. Everything else is audited by sample, with the same guarantee. This layer works today with Claude Code.

The guarantee

A stop is sampled only when the evidence says so.

If the real rate at which you would change a stop's answer is at or above its budget, the chance Jaynes ever makes that stop sampled is at most 5%, no matter how often it checks.

The usual shortcut, recomputing a confidence bound after every answer and switching the first time it fits, quietly breaks that promise. Run both and see.

Foundations

Built on results with proofs behind them.

  • methodThe method as a type

    Which steps are valid depends on the state the work is in.

    Strom and Yemini, "Typestate", IEEE TSE 12(1), 1986
  • methodYour stops as one side of a protocol

    An exchange between parties, checked as a type.

    Honda, "Types for Dyadic Interaction", CONCUR 1993
  • methodWrite access per step

    An agent can only touch what it was handed.

    Miller, "Robust Composition", PhD thesis, Johns Hopkins, 2006
  • method · safetyOne mechanism for gating, auditing and replay

    An action is an effect; a handler decides to run it, ask, record it, or replay it.

    Plotkin and Pretnar, "Handlers of Algebraic Effects", ESOP 2009
  • attentionA guarantee you can check after every answer

    Test martingales and e-values stay valid under continuous monitoring.

    Ramdas, Grünwald, Vovk and Shafer, Statistical Science 38(4), 2023; Grünwald, de Heide and Koolen, "Safe Testing", JRSS B 86(5), 2024
  • attention · nextFewer of your answers for the same guarantee

    A judge labels everything; your answers correct its bias.

    Angelopoulos, Bates, Fannjiang, Jordan and Zrnic, "Prediction-Powered Inference", Science, 2023
  • attention · nextAsk you about what is most uncertain

    Choose which items a person labels to reduce uncertainty the most.

    Zrnic and Candès, "Active Statistical Inference", ICML 2024
  • attention · nextNotice when a role gets worse

    Sequential change detection with a guarantee on false alarms.

    Shin, Ramdas and Rinaldo, "E-detectors", NEJSDS 2(2), 2024
